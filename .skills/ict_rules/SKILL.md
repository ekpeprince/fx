# ICT Trading Rules Cheat Sheet

This skill provides the core definitions and rules for evaluating market structure and trade setups using the ICT methodology.

## 1. Fair Value Gap (FVG)
- **Definition**: A three-candle formation where there is an imbalance in price delivery.
- **Bullish FVG**: The low of the 3rd candle does not overlap with the high of the 1st candle in an upward move.
- **Bearish FVG**: The high of the 3rd candle does not overlap with the low of the 1st candle in a downward move.
- **Rule**: Look for price to retrace into the FVG to rebalance before continuing the trend.

## 2. Order Block (OB)
- **Definition**: The last opposing candle before a strong move that breaks market structure.
- **Bullish OB**: The last down candle before a strong upward impulsive move.
- **Bearish OB**: The last up candle before a strong downward impulsive move.
- **Rule**: Institutional orders are accumulated here. Entry is typically at the open or mean threshold (50%) of the order block.

## 3. Liquidity Pools
- **Definition**: Areas where stop losses are clustered.
- **Buy Side Liquidity (BSL)**: Rests above old highs (resistance).
- **Sell Side Liquidity (SSL)**: Rests below old lows (support).
- **Rule**: The market seeks liquidity. A "turtle soup" or liquidity sweep occurs when price pierces these levels only to reverse.

## 4. Killzones
- **Definition**: Specific times of day with high volatility and institutional participation.
- **London Killzone**: 2:00 AM - 5:00 AM NY Time.
- **New York Killzone**: 7:00 AM - 10:00 AM NY Time.
- **Rule**: High probability trade setups often form during these windows. Avoid trading in the "dead zone" outside these hours.

## Market Structure Shift (MSS)
- **Definition**: A break of a significant swing high or low indicating a potential trend reversal.
- **Rule**: Wait for an MSS with displacement (energetic move leaving an FVG) before entering on a retracement to an OB or FVG.
